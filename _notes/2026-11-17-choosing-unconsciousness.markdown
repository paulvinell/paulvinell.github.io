**Choosing unconsciousness**

I've got a party trick, but I'm not sure you will be impressed. Alright, I'll tell you. It's actually quite short: Did you know that you can't choose unconsciousness? Don't believe me? Go ahead and try.

...

Could you do it? ... Did you think you could? I know I can't.

That was fun right? But it's not the whole story. I want to let you know that I know you. You're the regular type. You always think about this from the other – the usual – perspective. You're here, but you want to be there. You're living unconsciously most of the time and you know it. You scroll your phone a bit much, you get worked up at work (is that not what it's for?), and you fill in the silence in social situations like it was the gap between the train and the platform (very dangerous). But there's this one thing you can't have and you've made it your life mission to fix it. You want some presence. You're simple, you just want to be here now. Things would be so much better if you could just have some of that pres-ence. Please, just a little bit.

But see, the thing is that you can't. You can't have any of it. That's right – you can't have unconsciousness either. You're not a chooser, see? It's not up to you.

I'm honestly a bit insulted that you thought you could choose. You don't have that type of agency. Doesn't that make you feel like a bit of a dummy? You think that just because you spent a lot of time unconscious, that you're suddenly some kind of expert. Don't you think you think a bit highly of yourself, no? Who do you think you are? Could you please at least consider how it looks to other people before you act?
