**Authentic self**


There is this pervasive idea of an authentic self. An idea that at the core of 'you' there's another 'you' that is much cooler, nicer, and feels a whole lot better. Speaking to some people, it would seem like there's 'you:s' all the way down.

Who are you and what are you doing staring at me while I sleep? It is highly rude, and honestly, kind of intrusive. Don't look at me like that.

It's like that Animal Farm quote. All you:s are you, but some you:s are more you than others. Why are you creating this division? Conveniently, when you are scared or awkward, you can feel or say that you are not being your authentic self. Like Clark Kent hiding his real identity as Superman. You are only being your authentic self when you are cool and funny and people like you. How are you so sure it's not the other way around then? Why isn't the awkward you the authentic you? I don't know, it just seems awfully convenient that the real you is the version that you deem more acceptable.

It's genius, really. By introducing an 'authentic self' that you work to reveal, you create an internal maiden or princess to save, all while you still get to be the hero who rescues her. You get to be the rescuer and the rescuee. It's the best damn feel-good story ever invented.
