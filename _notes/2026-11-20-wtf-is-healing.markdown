**What the fuck is healing?**


What the fuck is h-h-he-healing? I thought I knew that word, once. It made the most sense when it referred to scrapes and bruises. Then it came to mean something else entirely. It came to mean psychological healing. It was a little (read: a lot) vaguer, but I kinda knew what it meant. You wouldn't feel so bad, was the promise. I don't know who promised me this exactly, but I sure was promised.

See the problem with healing is that it always comes later. Or you're standing in it but the end is on the other side. The other side of what? Exactly. Now look, don't get mad at me. I'm not saying it doesn't work, that you're wasting your time, or that there is no such thing as healing. There is, probably. The thing is, though, and this is the good part – it doesn't stop. Honestly, that could be the bad part too. You call it healing when you're actively traumatized, want empathy, or feel that you're doing below average for whatever reason. When you stop feeling extraordinarily bad, you might find that 'healing' starts feeling like an icky word. You'll however be delighted to learn that there's lots of fancy and unfancy alternatives. When you're out of the loop you call it self improvement. When you're cool and know psychology you call it self actualization, or individuation if you want to feel like you're better than other people or that you're part of a cool, exclusive club. If you're religious or spiritual it could be called your spiritual journey. Anyway – that's right – healing doesn't stop, it just changes name. Fuck, how far does this damn ladder go? It's like Jack and the Beanstalk. You tell me. Please.

Furthermore, I also find that we discuss the chaotic nature of healing way too rarely. I think a lot of people have a sense of what they would most like to work on. A behavior, mental attitude, or belief that they would like to "get to the bottom of." That's awesome, but I think regardless of whatever form of practice you're doing, just because you have an idea of what you would like to get in contact with, does not in fact mean that that issue is next in line, or necessarily even close to it. It's most obvious if one takes the example of going to a shrink – you want to really home in how hard it is to open your postbox and suddenly you're talking about your relationship to your mother. Talk about a bait and switch. But this isn't exclusive to any specific form of therapy. In somatic therapies I think it is the most obvious that you don't choose the order. I mean, you can try. It's just unlikely that the body will respond to work in and on areas that are not ready. I could overexplain this, but I think that if you've tried it, you know what I mean.

Healing is like chaotic neutral on an alignment chart. It's a lot like "cursed wish grants" – a concept I like a lot. However, before I explain that, I want to start with the best type of wish granting – the classic genie in a bottle. You rub the bottle, some genie (a Djinn, really) appears. He (or she!) gives you three wishes. You ask for an inflatable doll, a helicopter, and a cup of tea. Then you realize you ran out of wishes. The cursed wish grant is the type of deal you'd only take if it fulfilled a life goal of yours, and even then, that's a strong 'maybe'. Like wishing for fame, but cursed wish grant comes with the hidden crux that you'll die at 27. Or wanting to see the Titanic, but the curse is that you'll drown too (okay – implode – whatever, jerk).

Healing is often approximately in between. For instance, you wish to overcome your social anxiety. You're told your wish is granted, but when you go to take your new life out for a spin you're infuriated when you find out you still have it. You take it back to the dealer's office to complain. "This shit doesn't work – I was very uncomfortable!" "But you still socialized, right?" "Uh yeah, I guess." You still have the discomfort, but you're just better at tolerating it. If that sounds like an underwhelming sell, it's because it often is, in many ways. Yet, it can be completely life changing.
