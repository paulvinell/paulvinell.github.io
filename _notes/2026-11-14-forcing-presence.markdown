**Forcing presence**


I have noticed myself losing presence more often recently. I see that for once I'm thankfully not inclined to try to remain present. This is in some sense a relief, because for once I feel that I can allow that state of absence to just be.

For a long time I've been guilty of "forcing presence." I try not to force any specific state, but I will inevitably have a period of perceived great presence. I get attached to this feeling of presence and its elevated nature. Then I start forcing. I notice myself forcing and I see the fear of the 'sleeping' state. I find myself unable to stop forcing – which reminds me – can I really be forcing, if I feel that I am not intentionally choosing the forcing? Then it's just happening by itself, no? It seems as logical to me as saying that you accidentally did something on purpose. Or maybe that's exactly what forcing is, as an opposite of sorts to choosing. Where 'forcing' is just one head of 'sleep' or the unconscious state, and 'choosing' one of presence?

To me, it points to a sort of greater unfolding. If forcing is never truly chosen, but rather a product of the unconscious state – and if choosing can only ever truly be done in presence, how else could it be?

To bring it a bit more personal, I felt like nothing was happening or changing within me before I started trying. I think this is what contributes to the instinct to force. If nothing happened before I began trying, how could I expect something to happen if I quit it? My hope is that it's like pushing a boulder down a hill. Once it gets rolling, the unfolding of its path takes effort to stop or steer. However, my fear is that it's like Sisyphus.

[Enantiodromia][enantiodromia] comes to mind.

> Enantiodromia (Greek: enantios for "opposite" and dromos for "running course") is the psychological and philosophical principle that an extreme, one-sided attitude or tendency will eventually transform into its exact opposite.

What happens if I try to force myself to stay conscious? Probably counter to what I want in the long run.

I am for once sensing an increased happiness for my unconsciousness. Letting it be feels like rest, and I feel that I can have some trust in that it will return. It seems to be cyclic, after all.
