Fribord is an open source program for neurofeedback and biofeedback. Both work by measuring something your body does, such as your brain activity or your heart rate, and showing it back to you as a sound or a picture as it changes. By watching it, you learn to steer it, which is a way of training self-regulation. It has been a big help to me personally.

I made it because nearly all software for this is written for Windows and aimed at experts. I wanted something closer to plug and play.

It supports EEG biofeedback, HEG (blood flow in the forehead) with the Mendi headband, heart rate variability with HeartMath, and pupil biofeedback with a Tobii eye tracker.

Fribord is free software under the GPL, written in Python. It is a personal research project and not a medical device.

The code, the full list of supported devices and training screens, and how to run it are on GitHub: [paulvinell/fribord-eeg](https://github.com/paulvinell/fribord-eeg).
